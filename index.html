03 / THE CONNECTOR

Kath Baath.
Let’s talk.

“”

A space for voices.
A reason to gather.
A conversation that begins
at home, in Kashmir.

Kath Baath is a Srinagar-based nonprofit created to encourage discussion and discourse in Kashmir. Its public story centres on bringing young people together through conversation, debate, and creative expression.

My poetry is part of that story: I performed at Kalaam-e-Aam 2018, preserved in Kath Baath’s performance archive. It’s a meeting point for the artistic and community sides of my life.

RiSE also documented Kath Baath as a student-started discussion forum shortlisted by Ashoka Youth Venture for its ideas.
